2. Executive architecture principles
1. An initially authorized logical effect is the first attempt, admitted under existing admission and governance rules.
2. Any subsequent external write or delivery for the same logical effect is a separate attempt.
3. System-initiated subsequent attempts are prohibited.
4. UNKNOWN is not failure and does not authorize resending.
5. Missing receipts, timeouts, expired leases and unavailable databases are not proof of failure.
6. Evidence, authority, event identity, delivery identity and logical effect identity are separate concepts.
7. No silent overwrite, deletion, repair, adoption or cross-database reconciliation is permitted.
8. Eventual consistency is a design direction, not proof of convergence or atomicity.
9. Conservative default: refuse, display, hold.
10. Approval of a policy does not authorize its implementation or operation.

7. P1 — Zero Automatic Retry and attempt identity
P1-A: Zero Automatic Retry Definition (ZR-DEF)
OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-11-11
The initially authorized logical effect/request is the first attempt, admitted under existing admission and governance rules.

A subsequent attempt is any further external write or delivery for the same logical effect:

effect_type + logical_key

System-initiated subsequent attempts are prohibited.

The following do not authorize a subsequent attempt:
- Timer or scheduler activity, even when operator-configured.
- Lease expiry.
- Startup replay.
- Timeout.
- UNKNOWN status.
- Idempotency.
- Human action alone.
- The initial request's original authorization.
Each subsequent attempt requires its own specific, recorded, per-instance authorization under separate governance.

A late result from the original worker is not a new attempt.

Approval of P1-A does not authorize implementation or any subsequent attempt.
P1-B: Audit Sink scope
OWNER-APPROVED — OPTION X1
Zero Automatic Retry applies to Audit Sink delivery.

No automatic redelivery is authorized.

X2, a separate bounded deduplicated redelivery policy, is NOT APPROVED.

P1-B does not authorize modifying, disabling or operating existing S3-2 mechanisms.
P1-C1: Attempt-start boundary
OWNER-APPROVED — MARKER WRITTEN
An attempt is considered started when its attempt marker is durably written.

This is an accounting and governance boundary only.

It does not prove:
- Physical transmission.
- Provider receipt.
- Completion of the external effect.
- A successful external outcome.
No operational state machine or implementation is approved by this decision.
P1-C2: Audit event logical identity
OWNER-APPROVED — INDEPENDENT IDENTITY
Each internal Audit event has its own logical identity.

The identity is limited to internal audit-event identification.

It must not:
- Bypass P1-A.
- Authorize another external effect.
- Change the attempt-start boundary.
- Be treated as a delivery authorization.
8. P2 — UNKNOWN and external-effect lifecycle

P2-C2: Startup replay
OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-11-11
Automatic replay or resend of stored intents on restart is not authorized.

UNKNOWN is preserved.

A status check after restart does not authorize a resend.

Any later attempt is subject to P1-A.
P2-D1: UNKNOWN exit evidence
OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-11-11
A determination that an UNKNOWN external effect has reached a definitive outcome requires credible, traceable evidence linked to the exact operation.

Evidence must satisfy all five criteria:
1. Identity: clearly identifies the exact operation.
2. Source: identifies where the evidence originated.
3. Authenticity: provides a credible basis for trusting the evidence.
4. Integrity: has not been silently changed or corrupted.
5. Time and provenance: records when and how the evidence was obtained.
The following are not sufficient evidence of failure:
- Timeout.
- Lease expiry.
- Missing response.
- Missing receipt.
- Missing record.
- Database unavailability.
Provider acceptance is not equivalent to completion.

The following outcomes must remain distinct:
- ACCEPTED
- COMPLETED
- FAILED
- UNKNOWN
P2-D2: UNKNOWN decision authority
OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-11-11
Final UNKNOWN determination belongs to an authorized human using the required evidence.

A worker or scheduler cannot determine failure based on a guess, timeout or lease expiry.

The decision record must include:
- Approver identity.
- Decision time.
- Reason.
- Supporting evidence.
A human determination alone does not authorize resubmission or a subsequent external attempt.
P2-D3: Audit history preservation
OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-11-11
Audit history must not be silently overwritten or deleted.

Corrections and new determinations must be recorded as separate traceable events.

The original history remains preserved.
UNKNOWN and delivery-state separation
UNKNOWN external-effect status and UNKNOWN Audit Sink delivery status are distinct.

Evidence for the external effect does not automatically establish Audit Sink delivery.

Evidence for Audit Sink delivery does not automatically establish the external effect.
9. P3 — Audit Event identity and Audit Sink delivery
P3-A: Independent Audit Event Identity
OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-11-11
Each internal Audit event has its own durable audit_event_id.

The following identifiers have different meanings:
Identifier	Meaning
audit_event_id	Identity of the internal Audit event
outbox_key	Delivery identity
payload_digest	Payload integrity identity
effect_type + logical_key	Logical external-effect identity

Redelivery does not create a new logical Audit event.

An independent audit_event_id does not authorize a new external effect or bypass P1-A.

It must not be equated with outbox_key, payload digest or logical effect identity.
P3-B: Audit Sink delivery policy
OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-11-11
Automatic Audit Sink redelivery is not authorized.

Delivery state is separate from the internal Audit event status.

An UNKNOWN delivery state remains UNKNOWN unless supported by appropriate evidence and a separately authorized determination.

The following do not authorize automatic redelivery:
- Lease expiry.
- Startup replay.
- Restart.
- Timeout.
- Missing receipt.
- Deduplication.
- A previously recorded delivery intent.
Any subsequent Audit Sink delivery requires independent authorization under P3-C.

This approval does not authorize modifying, disabling or operating existing S3-2 mechanisms.
P3-C: Manual Audit redelivery authority
OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-11-11
Each subsequent Audit Sink delivery requires a specific, recorded, per-event/per-attempt human authorization.

The record must include:
- Approver identity.
- Time of authorization.
- Reason.
- Supporting evidence.
- Exact Audit event.
- Exact delivery attempt being authorized.
Authorization is single-use and cannot be reused for later attempts.

Audit redelivery permission never authorizes repetition of the original external effect.

This decision defines the authorization policy only. It does not authorize implementation or execution of redelivery.
